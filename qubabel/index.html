    The mathematics
    Each listener gets a distinct qubit in |Ψ⟩ = cos γ |00⟩ + exp(iφ) sin γ |11⟩. Their local basis is B = Rz(δ) Ry(2θ); interpretation probabilities follow |⟨ab| (B_A† ⊗ B_B†) |Ψ⟩|². A joint reading conditions the shared state. A new utterance prepares a fresh pair, while the updated personal bases persist within this run.
    Coupling γ=45° is maximally entangled; γ=0° is separable. Basis alignment and entanglement are different. At maximal coupling each listener’s marginal is 50/50, regardless of the other basis: entanglement changes joint correlations, not remote control of anyone’s reading.
    The backend job measures the current pair plus two reference Bell pairs with opposite phase in X bases. Reference predictions: phase 0 → agreement 100%; phase 180 → agreement 0%. An incoherent 00/11 mixture → agreement 50% in both cases.
    This is an invented semantic system, not a psychological model or a claim of quantum advantage. No automatic English sentiment inference. Local samples use classical randomness; backend results come from Qiskit.

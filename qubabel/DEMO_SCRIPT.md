# Suggested 2–3 minute demo

0:00–0:25 — Introduce Qubabel as an English-word conlang experiment. Two listeners hear the same factual sentence but derive subjective readings through their own bases.

0:25–0:55 — Set coupling to 45°. Compare aligned and differing listener angles. Point out that individual marginals stay 50/50 while agreement changes. Explain that basis alignment is distinct from entanglement.

0:55–1:25 — Hear several exchanges. Show the conversation log and the evolving basis angles and phase parameters. Explain that warm and guarded outcomes trigger invented classical adaptation rules; these affect future utterances. No objective facts are changed.

1:25–2:00 — Show phase-interference predictions. Contrast 100% and 0% agreement for opposite phases with the 50% mixture baseline. Run the six-qubit Qiskit job on the built-in simulator and show received counts. Label all displayed results as simulated.

2:00–2:30 — Show a free IonQ simulator run if verified. Explain the one-job-per-run design and documentation. Disclose AI assistance and the limits: not a psychology model, not quantum advantage, not automatic English intent recognition. End with the artistic goal: a language where relationships shape interpretation.

Do not present unexecuted platform runs as completed results.
